# Indian Knowledge System - Module 1

## <u>Definition of IKS</u>  
---

IKS includes a wide range of disciplines, philosophies, science & arts,
cultural practices that have been developed over a millennium. The Indian Knowledge System is preserved through traditional written text and practical appreciations.


## <u>Features of IKS</u>  
---

- Holistic approach to life.  
- Philosophical foundations.  
- Spiritual wisdom & Textual traditions.  
- Scientific Contributions.  
- Cultural diversity & Artistic expression.  
- Yoga & Meditation practices.  
- Ethical & Social values.  
- Education & Transmission of knowledge.  
- Impact & Legacy.  
- Modern Relevance & Adaption.

## <u>Ancient IKS - Home Education</u>  
---

It is said that education of an individual starts at home, since ancient times, within any family, children observe their parents and relatives, naturally absorbing essential life lessons, moral values, and social behaviors through daily interactions. Through shared family traditions, cultural rituals, and stories passed down through generations.  
This phenomenon is called as <u>Home Education</u>

### <u> Characteristics of Home Education </u>  
- It is **Informal** in nature  
- It imparts the foundations of learning such as Role of Parents & Elders.  
- It helps development of practical & social development.  

### <u> Methods and Approaches to Home Education </u>  
- Story telling and oral foundations.  
- Observational learning.  
- Rituals & Religious Practices.  
- Transmission of cultural heritage.  
- Socialization of cultural value.
